Privacy

  • The simulator runs entirely in your browser. There is no account and no server-side storage.
  • Saved runs are kept in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB), and your language choice in localStorage. They never leave your device; clearing site data removes them.
  • Files you export (CSV, saves) are downloaded directly to your device.
  • If this copy of the site shows ads, they are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services; you can manage ad personalisation in Google's ad settings.

What this is

An educational and research simulation. Societies, names, rulers and events are generated by the model; they are not historical records. Regional factors (for example slower state formation in some regions) are model choices and can be changed in the parameter panel.

Data

  • Coastlines, rivers, lakes and region names: Natural Earth (public domain). Made with Natural Earth.
  • Elevation: ETOPO 10 arc-minute ice-surface relief (NOAA), via the pyvista vtk-data repository.
  • Present climate: Köppen-Geiger classification, Rubel et al. 2016 (Meteorologische Zeitschrift, doi:10.1127/metz/2016/0816), via the kgcpy package; Kottek, M., J. Grieser, C. Beck, B. Rudolf and F. Rubel (2006), Meteorol. Z., 15, 259-263.
  • Orbital insolation: Laskar et al. 2004 (La2004), via the inso package.
  • Ice volume: LR04 benthic δ18O stack, Lisiecki and Raymo 2005, via pyleoclim.
  • Plate boundaries: Bird 2003, PB2002 (Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems); GeoJSON from github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates, Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By 1.0).
  • Volcanoes: volcano list from the plotly/datasets repository.

3D models and libraries

  • 3D buildings, trees, rocks, vehicles, siege engines and people: Kenney asset packs (CC0, public domain). Modern buildings, factories and the tank are simple models made for this site.
  • three.js 0.160 (MIT licence), loaded from the jsDelivr CDN only when the 3D map layer is first shown.

Apart from three.js for the 3D layer, the page is plain HTML and JavaScript.
